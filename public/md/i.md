# KeyBurn inbox link

This is the private inbox of a KeyBurn secret request. It belongs to the person who asked for the secret: it shows whether an answer has arrived, opens it once (the answer is then deleted from the server) and can cancel the request. Its key is the part of the link after `#`. HTTP clients never send that part, so this response can't show the inbox.

## How to open it

Open the full link in a web browser. The answer is decrypted on your device.

AI agents: the osss tool can't open inbox links yet. Ask the person to open the link in their browser. If the answer is a secret you need, they can share it with you as a KeyBurn secret link, which osss can open: see https://keyburn.dev/agents.md.
